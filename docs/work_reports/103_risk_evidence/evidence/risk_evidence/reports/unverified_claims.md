# Unverified and Prohibited Claims

This evidence does not verify that RiskEvidence eliminates hallucinations, detects all malicious agents, guarantees complete security, or proves real-world fraud accuracy. The synthetic corpus does not represent all banking, drone, voting, or AI-agent attacks. OpenBSD sandboxing alone does not prove Quarantine correctness. External-oracle evidence is not inherently trustworthy. The extension is optional, its thresholds are not universally optimal, and the measurements do not establish O(1) complexity for the complete Quarantine subsystem.

OpenBSD formatting and clippy compliance were not re-evaluated on-host because the packaged toolchain lacks those components. Ubuntu performed both checks on the same source commit.
